Discover wonders worth seeing

Abu Simbel Temples

Aswan, Nubia

Two massive rock temples built by Pharaoh Ramesses II, featuring colossal statues and intricate hieroglyphics

Alexandria National Museum

Alexandria, Tariq Al-Horreya St

The museum contains around 1,800 artifacts that narrate the story of Alexandria and Egypt

Coptic Museum

Cairo, Masr AL Kadima

The Coptic Museum houses the largest collection of Coptic artifacts in the world

Dahshur Pyramids

Giza, Dahshur

Ancient pyramid complex including the Bent Pyramid and Red Pyramid, showcasing early pyramid construction

Egyptian Museum

Cairo, Tahrir Square

It is the oldest archaeological museum in the Middle East, housing over 170,000 artefacts

Grand Egyptian Museum

Giza, Cairo-Alexandria Desert Road

The world's largest archaeological museum dedicated to a single civilization, showcasing Egypt's great heritage

Giza Necropolis

Giza, Al Haram

Home to the Great Pyramid, the Pyramid of Khafre, and the Pyramid of Menkaure and the Great Sphinx. A site of great historical significance

Kalabsha Temple

Aswan ,Bab al-Kalabsha

The largest free-standing temple in Lower Nubia. Multiple cultures have left their mark on the temple of Kalabsha

Karnak Temple Complex

Luxor, Karnak

Comprises a vast mix of temples, pylons, chapels, and other buildings. It is the largest religious complex ever built by humans

Kom El Shoqafa

Alexandria, Karmouz District

One of Alexandria’s main attractions. As they are wonderful examples of the influence of Ancient Egyptian art on Roman art

Luxor Museum

Luxor, Kornish Al Nile

Known as one of Egypt’s best cultural sites. It is praised for its mix of research and visitor-friendly experiences

Luxor Temple

Luxor, East Bank

Located in the heart of Luxor, this temple is a magnificent example of ancient Egyptian architecture and religious devotion

Manial Palace Museum

Cairo, Manial

This Cairo palace blends Islamic and European architectural styles and houses royal artifacts. Visitors can explore the lush gardens and richly decorated rooms

Memphis Open-Air Museum

Giza, Mit Rahina

An open-air site showcasing colossal statues and remnants of ancient Memphis, the old capital of Egypt. It provides a glimpse into the grandeur of Pharaonic civilization

Mummification Museum

Luxor, Kornish Al Nile

This museum explains the mummification process in ancient Egypt. It displays tools, embalmed remains, and interactive exhibits

Museum of Islamic Art

Cairo, Bab El-Khalq

Features one of the world’s largest collections of Islamic art, including textiles, ceramics, and manuscripts. Its architecture itself is a work of art

National Museum of Egyptian Civilization

Cairo, Fustat

Chronicles Egypt’s history from prehistoric times to the present. Famous for hosting the royal mummies parade

Nubian Museum

Aswan, El Numaria

The museum’s design reflects traditional Nubian architecture and it showcases beautiful Nubian culture

Philae Temple

Aswan, Agilkia Island

An island-based temple complex, includes many structures dating predominantly to the Ptolemaic Period

Qaitbay Citadel

Alexandria, Eastern Harbor

A 15th-century fortress built on the site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria. It offers stunning views of the Mediterranean

Royal Jewelry Museum

Alexandria, Zizenia

Displays the jewelry, crowns, and personal items of the royal Muhammad Ali family. Highlights the opulence of Egypt’s royal history

Saqqara Necropolis

Giza, Saqqara

The vast burial ground near Giza, illustrates the evolution of pyramid construction and ancient funerary practices

Sadat Museum

Cairo, Abbassia

Dedicated to former President Anwar Sadat, showcasing personal items, photographs, and historical documents

Temple of Hatshepsut

Luxor, Deir el-Bahari

Temple honoring Pharaoh Hatshepsut. It's terraces and colonnades are celebrated for their architectural elegance

Tomb of Tutankhamun

Luxor, Valley of the Kings

The famous boy king’s tomb in the Valley of the Kings, renowned for its treasures and artifacts. It provides insight into 18th Dynasty royal life

Valley of the Kings

Luxor, West Bank

Vast burial ground for pharaohs and nobles of the New Kingdom. It's tombs reveal ancient Egyptian religious beliefs about the afterlife

Valley of the Queens

Luxor, West Bank

A necropolis near Luxor for queens and royal children. The tombs feature intricate wall paintings depicting daily life and religious rituals
